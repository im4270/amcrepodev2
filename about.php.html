Set Clear Financial Goals

P1

Define your purpose: wealth creation, retirement, passive income, or short-term gains. Set a time horizon (short, medium, or long term).

Understand Your Risk Tolerance

P2

Decide how much risk you can handle.

Conservative → Stable, low-risk stocks

Moderate → Balanced portfolio

Aggressive → High-growth stocks

Open a Trading & Demat Account

P3

Choose a registered broker and complete KYC.

In India, ensure the broker is regulated by the Securities and Exchange Board of India.

In the U.S., brokers are overseen by the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission.

Research Before Investing

P4

Analyze:

Company fundamentals (profit, revenue, debt)

Industry trends

Market conditions

Avoid investing based only on tips or rumors.

Diversify Your Portfolio

P5

Don’t put all money in one stock.

Spread investments across different sectors and asset classes to reduce risk.

Monitor & Invest for the Long Term

P6

Track performance regularly.

Stay patient — long-term investing usually benefits from compounding growth.

Avoid emotional decisions during market volatility.
