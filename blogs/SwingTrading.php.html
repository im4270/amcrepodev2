Swing Trading Definition

Learn how swing trading works, its strategies, benefits, risks, and how traders capture short-term market momentum.

What is Swing Trading?

Swing trading is a short-to-medium term trading strategy where traders attempt to capture price swings in stocks, indices, or other financial instruments over a few days to several weeks.

Definition: Swing trading focuses on identifying short-term momentum shifts and profiting from temporary price movements within a broader trend.

Unlike intraday trading, swing traders hold positions overnight and sometimes for multiple sessions to maximize potential gains.

Key Characteristics

  • Holding period: 2 days to several weeks
  • Technical analysis driven
  • Trend-following strategy
  • Requires disciplined risk management
