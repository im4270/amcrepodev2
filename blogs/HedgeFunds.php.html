How Hedge Funds Work

Understanding hedge fund structure, strategies, risks, and comparison with PMS & AIF in India.

What is a Hedge Fund?

A hedge fund is a privately managed pooled investment vehicle that uses advanced strategies such as leverage, derivatives, short selling, and arbitrage to generate high returns. These funds typically cater to accredited investors and institutions.

Hedge funds aim to generate absolute returns — meaning they try to make profits regardless of whether markets are rising or falling.

They commonly follow the “2 and 20” fee structure — 2% management fee and 20% performance fee.

Major Hedge Fund Strategies

1. Long / Short Equity

Buying undervalued stocks and short selling overvalued stocks.

2. Global Macro

Trading based on global economic trends and macro data.

3. Event Driven

Investing during mergers, acquisitions, restructurings.

4. Arbitrage

Capturing pricing inefficiencies across markets.
