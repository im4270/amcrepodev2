1. Introduction

AMC Financial Service (“AMC,” “Company,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting the privacy and security of our clients’ personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our trading, brokerage, and related financial services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal information:

A. Personal Identification Information
Full name, Date of birth, Government-issued ID, Passport/Driver’s License, Tax identification number

B. Contact Information
Residential address, Email address, Phone number

C. Financial Information
Bank account details, Payment card details, Trading history, Investment preferences, Income and net worth information

D. Technical Information
IP address, Device information, Browser type, Login data, Cookies and tracking technologies

E. Compliance Information
KYC documents, AML verification data, Politically Exposed Person (PEP) status, Sanctions screening results

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to: open and manage your trading account, verify identity (KYC/AML compliance), execute trades and transactions, process payments and withdrawals, provide customer support, improve platform performance, prevent fraud and unauthorized activity, and meet legal and regulatory obligations. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data based on: contractual necessity, legal and regulatory obligations, legitimate business interests, or your consent (where required).

5. Sharing of Information

We may share your information with: regulatory authorities, clearing houses and exchanges, banking and payment providers, identity verification providers, IT and cloud service providers, legal and compliance advisors. All third parties are required to maintain confidentiality and data security.

6. Data Security

AMC Financial Service implements appropriate technical and organizational safeguards, including data encryption, secure servers, firewalls, multi-factor authentication, and restricted employee access. While we strive to protect your information, no electronic transmission is completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as your account remains active, as required by financial regulations, or as necessary to resolve disputes or enforce agreements. Regulatory retention periods may range from 5–10 years depending on jurisdiction.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your data, object to processing, or withdraw consent. Requests may be submitted via the contact information below.

9. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website and trading platform may use cookies to enhance user experience, remember login sessions, analyze website traffic, and improve security. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not function properly.

10. International Data Transfers

If we transfer your data outside your country of residence, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

11. Children’s Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

12. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted on our website with a revised effective date. Continued use of our services after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

13. Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact:
AMC Financial Service

Amc Financial Services – Privacy Commitment

Your privacy is our priority. Below is a summary of how we manage your information.

• Trust First: We safeguard the data of all current and past clients with the highest level of integrity.

• Seamless Communication: When you sign up, you agree to receive calls, emails, and SMS from us. This applies even if you are registered under DND.

• Zero Spam Policy: We do not sell your data. We only use it to provide services, send newsletters, and gather feedback through surveys.

• Legal Compliance: If required by law or a regulatory body, we may disclose necessary information to government authorities or security issuers without prior notice.
